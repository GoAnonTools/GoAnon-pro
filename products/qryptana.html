What it should communicate

Qryptana gives GoAnon a communication pillar. It should feel practical and human: private conversations without building another identity profile.

Core direction

No surveillance economy. No identity dependency. Just private communication.

  • No phone number.
  • No email.
  • No App Store.
  • No Play Store.
  • No public user ID.
  • No mandatory push dependency.
  • No platform-controlled distribution.

Qryptana chooses independence over maximum convenience. It is a mobile-first web messenger for private, store-free, identity-light communication — built for people who accept a little friction in exchange for less platform dependency and less exposure.
