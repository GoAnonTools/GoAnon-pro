Good Compartment Rules

  • Use one compartment per platform type — never reuse identities across categories.
  • Do not reuse your primary email in any compartment — use alias services or plus-addressing instead.
  • Do not reuse usernames across compartments — each one should be unique and unlinkable.
  • Do not mix personal and anonymous accounts — keep contexts strictly separated.
  • Save the compartment in Vaultana if you want to keep it for future reference.
  • Use Keyana to derive a unique password for this compartment — never reuse passwords.
  • Reveal less by default — only share what the platform strictly requires.
  1. 1 You choose a compartment purpose, risk level, style, and optionally provide a keyword or platform name. Maskana uses these to shape the identity profile.
  2. 2 A cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues) selects from local word lists — first-name-like aliases, adjectives, nouns, tech terms, and abstract words. No Math.random() is used anywhere.

  5. 5 Nothing leaves your browser. No server, no sync, no storage by default, no analytics. Your compartments exist only in the current session unless you export or copy them.
