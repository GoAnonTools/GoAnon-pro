Private age proof

Prove your age. Not your identity.

Verify is a GoAnon browser extension being built to let websites confirm age eligibility without receiving your ID document, exact birthdate, face scan, wallet identifier, or browsing trail.

Coming soon Privacy model

Verify is not a public browser-store release yet. The extension is in private alpha while the EUDI-compatible wallet path and privacy model are tested.

What websites should learn

The goal is simple: a website should learn whether you meet an age requirement, not who you are.

Shared

Only what is necessary

  • Age eligibility, for example over 18 = yes.
  • A cryptographic proof or wallet presentation.
  • A short-lived challenge bound to the requesting website.
  • A privacy label explaining the proof method.
Not shared

No identity disclosure

  • Name.
  • Exact birthdate.
  • ID document or passport scan.
  • Face scan or biometric data.
  • Wallet identifier.
  • Browsing activity to issuer, government, or GoAnon.

Current status

Verify is being built carefully because age verification can either protect dignity or become surveillance infrastructure. GoAnon will only enable official wallet support after the privacy checks are clear.

Digital Dignity checks

Official wallet support will only be enabled after confirming no issuer or government callback during normal proof use, no exact birthdate sharing, no stable wallet identifier, and no GoAnon tracking relay.

For websites

Verify is designed for cooperating websites that explicitly request a privacy-preserving age proof. It is not a bypass tool and does not manipulate third-party platforms.

  • Ask only for age eligibility, not full identity.
  • Use a website-bound challenge to prevent replay.
  • Show users what is shared and what is not shared.
  • Avoid central logs of where people verify age.
Coming soon: Verify is currently in private alpha inside the GoAnon ecosystem. Public installation links will be added when the Chromium and Firefox extension releases are ready.
