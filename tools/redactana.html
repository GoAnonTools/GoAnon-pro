Redactana scans your text using regular expressions to detect common patterns of personally identifiable information: email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, credit card numbers, national ID patterns, URLs, dates, API keys/tokens, and cryptocurrency addresses. Each category can be toggled on or off. Detection is approximate — always review the redacted output before sharing. Your text is never sent to any server.
